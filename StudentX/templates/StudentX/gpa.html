How is GPA calculated?

GPA is calculated using the weighted average of grade points based on course credits.

GPA = Σ(Credits × Grade Point) ÷ Σ(Credits)

*Percentage conversion varies by university. The displayed percentage uses GPA × 10 as a general reference.
