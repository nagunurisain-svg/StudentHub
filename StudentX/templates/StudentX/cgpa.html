How is CGPA calculated?

CGPA is calculated using the credit-weighted average of all semester SGPAs.

CGPA = Σ(SGPA × Credits) ÷ Σ(Credits)

The percentage conversion shown above uses CGPA × 10 as a general conversion. Your university may use a different formula.
