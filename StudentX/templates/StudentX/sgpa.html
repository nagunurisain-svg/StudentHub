How is SGPA calculated?

SGPA is calculated using the weighted average of grade points based on subject credits.

SGPA = Σ(Credit × Grade Point) ÷ Σ(Credits)

*Percentage conversion may differ between universities. The displayed percentage uses SGPA × 10 as a general reference.
